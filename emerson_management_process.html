Layer 5 — operational

President's Operating Report (POR)

What is it?
Financial reporting package for Emerson Business Units. Basis for internal and external communication.
Frequency
Updated quarterly by Business Units. Updates financial expectations for each quarter and fiscal year.
Attendees
Business Unit finance and leadership teams. Results feed up to Corporate.
Content
Uniform financial analysis of results and projections. Communicates BU commitment to financial targets.
Expectations
  • Coherent, challenging, and realistic sales plan
  • Reasonable profit leverage and deleverage assumptions
  • Headcount plans consistent with sales outlook
  • Focus on asset productivity — TWC and Capex
  • Continuous improvement in margin and asset productivity
Layer 5 — operational

President's Council

What is it?
Forum for discussing performance within a year by comparing POR to actual performance and near-term outlooks.
Frequency
Quarterly reviews; annual expectations monitored throughout.
Attendees
Corporate: COO & CFO. Business: Business Unit Leaders, President, and CFO.
Content
Monitors 4–5 fiscal year strategic imperatives: price/NMI, inventory, restructuring, growth programs.
Expectations
  • Financial plans must have integrity
  • Commitment of the management team
  • Alignment with Emerson's overall strategic goals
Layer 4 — operational

Business Unit Profit Review

What is it?
One-day review, typically at the business unit site, focused on driving margin, cash flow, and ROTC.
Frequency
Annual; presentations scheduled Q1–Q2 of following year. Exhibits submitted in summer.
Attendees
COO, Business Unit Leaders, President, and key managers.
Content
Key profit levers: price, material containment, supply chain productivity, footprint optimization, SG&A.
Expectations
  • Thoughtful analysis to generate plans and exhibit packages
  • Plans linked to Growth Conference plans
  • Intentional monitoring of success/failure of past actions
Layer 4 — operational

Growth Conference

What is it?
Focused discussion between business management and OCE on growth levers and investments needed.
Frequency
Annual event coinciding with the Profit Review. Exhibits submitted in August prior.
Attendees
Enterprise: CEO, OCE representative, VP Strategy Planning. Business: Leaders and President.
Content
Growth exhibits outlining top initiatives, incremental growth forecasts, KPIs, and rationale.
Expectations
  • Exhibits must cover at least 70% of incremental growth
  • Must be submitted with the profit plan and tie together
  • Presentation must align with original submission
Layer 4 — operational

Organization Review

What is it?
1–2 day review at Corporate. Ensures talent is aligned to deliver the business strategy.
Frequency
Business Units present every year.
Attendees
Emerson CEO, OCE, Business Unit President, BU HR Leader, and Corporate HR.
Content
Org charts, organization issues, planning charts, high potential lists, diversity dashboard.
4 Review Components
  • Business Strategy → Critical Roles
  • Key Organizational Issues
  • Leadership Performance & Succession Planning
  • Talent Review — identifying high-potential talent
Layer 2 — senior leadership

Strategy Reviews / OCE Offsite

What is it?
Multi-day offsite strategic discussion among top-level management, including strategy reviews consolidated with the OCE Offsite.
Frequency
Once per year. Strategy reviews are included as part of the OCE meeting.
Attendees
CEO, OCE representatives, Business Unit leaders, functional leaders.
Content
Strategy for key growth platforms, investment allocations, risk/reward assessments, M&A, market dynamics.
Layer 1 — enterprise

Corporate Planning Conference (CPC)

What is it?
One to two day strategic communication presentation by Corporate in North America, Europe, and Asia.
Frequency
Once per year.
Attendees
~700 top leaders across corporate, all businesses, and world areas.
Content
FY results, strategic business review and outlook, culture/portfolio/execution focus programs, annual awards.
Enterprise governance

Board Meetings

What is it?
Formal governance meetings with Emerson's Board of Directors, reviewing major strategic and financial decisions.
Frequency
Twice per year.
Example Topics
FY25 orders progression, R&D spend analysis, incremental growth plans, strategic business unit updates.
Layer 3 — functional

Technology, IT, Procurement & Logistics

What is it?
Cross-functional review layer spanning all business units — ensuring enterprise-wide alignment on technology, procurement strategy, supply chain, and logistics execution.
Role in the hierarchy
Sits between the operational base forums and senior leadership reviews — translating execution insights upward and enterprise direction downward.
Key focus areas
Digital transformation, IT infrastructure, indirect material management, supplier development, nearshoring and supply chain resilience.
Connection to value creation
Factory automation, Oracle roadmap, material cost containment, and tariff mitigation are channeled through this layer.
Layer 5 — operational

Country Growth Review

What is it?
Focused review of growth strategy and performance at the country level, covering key markets in Asia Pacific, EMEA, and the Americas.
Attendees
World Area leaders, country managers, and relevant Business Unit leadership.
Content
Market dynamics, customer penetration, distributor performance, local growth initiatives, and geopolitical risk factors.
Examples
APAC reviews covering China decline analysis, Taiwan growth roadmap, India supplier development, and Japan distribution strategy.
